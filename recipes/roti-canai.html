Roti Canai

Just your everyday simple roti canai in the morning

Ingredients

Dough

  • 3 cups (approx. 375 to 400g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp ghee or vegetable oil

Resting and frying

  • Extra vegetable oil (for coating the dough)
  • Ghee or margarine for pan-frying

Instructions

  1. Make the Dough:Combine flour, salt, and sugar in a large bowl. Whisk warm water, egg, condensed milk, and melted ghee together, then slowly pour into the flour while mixing. Knead for 8 to 10 minutes until smooth, soft, and elastic.
  2. Rest Overnight: Divide the dough into 6 to 8 equal balls. Coat every ball thoroughly in vegetable oil (or submerge in oil/ghee inside an airtight container) and let rest for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Stretch & Fold: Oil your work surface and hands. Flatten one dough ball, then gently pull and stretch the edges outward until the dough becomes paper-thin and translucent. Fold the edges inward into a square envelope (or gather into a long rope and coil into a spiral).
  4. Fry & Fluff:Cook on a medium-hot flat pan with a little ghee for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown with crispy blisters. Remove from the pan and immediately clap the hot roti between both hands to separate and fluff up the flaky layers. Serve hot with curry or dhal.
